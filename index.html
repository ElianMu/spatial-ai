What is real vs. approximated

Sensing: a 360° LiDAR (0.18 m, 12 m) and a 105° RGB-D camera (0.95 m, 6.5 m) are simulated by 3D ray casting against the building's colliders, with range noise. MOMO never reads the floor plan.

Mapping: real log-odds occupancy-grid mapping at 0.25 m, frontier-based exploration and A* planning on MOMO's own map, with a footprint sweep.

Pose: noisy wheel odometry corrected by correlative scan-to-map matching is estimated and scored. The map itself is registered with the simulator pose ("mapping with known poses"); this is not full SLAM and has no loop closure.

Recognition: objects are detected with an oracle-labelled detector that still needs line of sight, field of view and size-dependent range; it returns true categories and persistent ids. Room types, zones and spatial relations are inferred from those observations.

Manipulation: grasp points, handles and articulation axes are modelled for future work; MOMO has no arm, so it cannot open doors or drawers.
